## Why Wayanad?

Wayanad is a stunning hill district in Kerala, known for its lush forests, misty mountains, spice plantations, and rich biodiversity. Located in the Western Ghats, this pristine destination offers:

- 🌄 **Breathtaking Landscapes**: Rolling hills, dense forests, and picturesque valleys
- 🌡️ **Pleasant Climate**: Cool weather year-round, especially from October to May
- 🏛️ **Rich Heritage**: Ancient caves, tribal culture, and historical temples
- 🦁 **Wildlife**: Part of the Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve with diverse flora and fauna

## Explore Wayanad's Top Attractions

### Adventure & Trekking
- **Chembra Peak** (2,100m): Wayanad's highest peak featuring a heart-shaped lake en route to the summit
- **Edakkal Caves**: Ancient caves at 1,200m altitude featuring 6,000-year-old stone age carvings
- **Meenmutty Falls**: Three-tiered waterfall perfect for trekking enthusiasts
- **Neelimala View Point**: Stunning viewpoint offering panoramic vistas

### Nature & Wildlife
- **Wayanad Wildlife Sanctuary**: Part of the Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve, home to elephants, tigers, and diverse species
- **Tholpetty Wildlife Sanctuary**: Jeep safaris to spot elephants, deer, and big cats
- **Pakshipathalam Bird Sanctuary**: Haven for rare bird species including the Edible-nest Swift
- **Kuruva Island**: Freshwater island in Kabini River surrounded by evergreen forests

### Waterfalls & Lakes
- **Pookode Lake**: Natural freshwater lake shaped like India's map, surrounded by forests
- **Soochippara (Sentinel Rock) Falls**: Popular leisure destination 22km from Kalpetta
- **Kanthanpara Waterfalls**: Hidden gem between lush tea estates
- **Karlad Lake**: Serene lake perfect for picnics and relaxation

### Dams & Engineering Marvels
- **Banasura Sagar Dam**: Largest earthen dam in India and second-largest in Asia
- **Karapuzha Dam**: Scenic dam with gushing water and islands for aquatic birds

### Cultural & Heritage Sites
- **Thirunelli Temple**: Ancient temple in Brahmagiri hills with 30 granite columns, dedicated to Lord Vishnu
- **Wayanad Heritage Museum**: Largest archaeological museum in Kerala
- **Sulthan Bathery**: Historic town with Jain temples and pre-historic caves
- **Chain Tree (Lakkidi)**: Famous Ficus tree with fascinating local legends

### Spice Plantations & Agriculture
Wayanad is renowned for its spice plantations featuring coffee, tea, pepper, cardamom, and vanilla. Our stays offer:
- Guided plantation tours
- Learn about sustainable farming practices
- Experience the spice trade heritage
- Fresh farm-to-table cuisine

## Best Time to Visit

The ideal time to visit Wayanad is from October to May when the weather is cool and perfect for sightseeing and outdoor activities. The monsoon season (June-September) brings heavy rainfall, transforming the region into a lush green paradise.

## Getting to Wayanad

- **Nearest Airport**: Calicut International Airport (95 km)
- **Nearest Railway Station**: Kozhikode (70 km)
- **Road Connectivity**: Well-connected to major cities in Kerala and Karnataka
- **Popular Route**: Via Thamarassery Ghat (9 hairpin bends with stunning views)
