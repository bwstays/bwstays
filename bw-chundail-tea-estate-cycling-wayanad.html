Cycling at Chundail estate

View places within 15 Kms
6 km from Kalpetta
  • Timing: not published
  • Transportation: Public
  • Seasons to Visit: Summer (February-May), Winter (October-January)

  • Contact: not published

Chundail Tea Estate: Chundale, a few kilometres south of Kalpetta, is surrounded by tea estates; Harrisons Malayalam lists a Chundale Estate (Chundale Post, 673 123) among its Wayanad gardens. These are working private plantations, so visitors keep to public roads unless an estate offers a tour. No visitor hours or fees are published.

Sources: harrisonsmalayalam.com, en.wikipedia.org.
