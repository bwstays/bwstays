Wayanad, Kerala, is known for its Coffee plantations, particularly Robusta, which accounts for a significant portion of the state's coffee production. These plantations, often nestled among shade trees, contribute significantly to the local economy and are a popular destination for tourists seeking to experience the coffee-making process
