Kanthanpara Falls

View places within 15 Kms
18 km from Kalpetta
  • Timing: 9:00 am – 4:30 pm
  • Transportation: Public
  • Seasons to Visit: Summer (February-May), Monsoon (June-September), Winter (October-January)
  • Type: Falls
  • Fee: Adult INR 40, child INR 30
  • Duration :
  • Holiday: NA
  • Contact: +91 493 621 5342, +91 75938 92953

Kanthanpara Falls: Kanthanpara is a smaller waterfall, about 100 ft high, on the Vaduvanchal road 8 km from Meppadi and about 19 km from Kalpetta, close to the Meppadi range of the South Wayanad Forest Division. DTPC mentions an optional trek of about two hours along the Kanthanpara river.

Sources: dtpcwayanad.com.
