Mailadippara Romantic

View places within 15 Kms
3 km from Kalpetta
  • Timing: not published
  • Transportation: Public
  • Seasons to Visit: Summer (February-May), Monsoon (June-September), Winter (October-January)
  • Type: Romantic
  • Fee : not published
  • Duration :
  • Holiday: NA
  • Contact: not published

Mailadippara: Myladippara is a cliff-like hill just east of the Kalpetta Civil Station, next to the national highway bypass. It is reached by a short walk up from the road and is visited for its view over Kalpetta town. No official timing, fee or management body is published for it.

Sources: en.wikipedia.org.
