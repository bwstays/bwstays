Pulpally Sitadevi Temple

View places within 15 Kms
30 km from Kalpetta
  • Timing: 5:00 am – 12:30 pm, 5:30 pm – 7:30 pm
  • Transportation: Public
  • Seasons to Visit: Summer (February-May), Winter (October-January)
  • Type: Pilgrimage
  • Fee : not published
  • Duration :
  • Holiday: NA
  • Contact: +91 493 624 1175

Pulpally Sitadevi temple: The temple stands about 280 m from Pulpally bus stand and is managed by the Malabar Devaswom Board, with a member of the Kuppathode family as trustee. It is one of the few temples in Kerala dedicated to Seetha, and it also has idols of her sons Lava and Kusa, linking it to local Ramayana traditions.

Sources: dtpcwayanad.com, en.wikipedia.org.
