Terms of Service

To make a booking with bw stays& simply fill in the online reservation form or download the reservation form and call or mail to us. Spaces will be confirmed in order of receipt and upon payment of deposit.

Once your reservation has been confirmed it is necessary to make full payment at least 90 days prior to the tour commencement date. Payment for the tour deposit may be made by Visa, Mastercard, cash deposit, internet banking or International Money Transfer.

Payment for the balance of the tour fee may be made by cash deposit, internet banking or International Money Transfer, Visa or Mastercard.

Fees are quoted in United States dollars or Indian Rupees and are inclusive of the GST (18%).

We regret that personal or bank cheques are not accepted. We reserve the right to cancel your booking if payment is not received when due, wherein the Cancellations and Refunds clause, listed below, shall be effected.

Cancellations & Refunds

To cancel or change a booking, write to us from the email address or contact form used for the booking, with your name and stay dates. The date and time we receive your request is the cancellation date.

Standard dates. Cancelled 15 or more days before check-in: full refund of the amount paid, less any payment-gateway or bank charges. Cancelled 7 to 14 days before check-in: 50% of the amount paid is refunded. Cancelled less than 7 days before check-in, or no-show: no refund.

Holiday and peak-season stays. A stay is a holiday booking if any night falls on a public holiday, a festival weekend or between 20 December and 5 January. Cancelled 30 or more days before check-in: full refund, less payment-gateway or bank charges. Cancelled 15 to 29 days before check-in: 50% refund. Cancelled less than 15 days before check-in, or no-show: no refund.

Changing dates. You may move a confirmed booking to other dates once, free of charge, up to 7 days before check-in, subject to availability. Any difference in the rate is payable.

Early departure. Unused nights are not refunded.

If we cancel. If bw stays cancels a booking, you receive a full refund of everything you paid, or you may choose new dates.

Weather and events beyond our control. If floods, landslides, road closures or similar events make the stay impossible, we will offer you alternative dates.

Refund timing. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 to 10 working days.

What is the cancellation and refund policy at BW Stays Wayanad?

Standard dates: cancel 15 or more days before check-in for a full refund (less payment-gateway or bank charges), 7 to 14 days before for a 50% refund, and less than 7 days before or no-show for no refund. Holiday and peak-season stays (public holidays, festival weekends, 20 December to 5 January): 30 or more days before for a full refund, 15 to 29 days before for 50%, and less than 15 days before or no-show for no refund. You can move a confirmed booking once, free of charge, up to 7 days before check-in, subject to availability. Refunds are paid back to the original payment method within 7 to 10 working days. Full details: https://www.bwstays.com/footer.html#cancellation

What does the villa cost per night?

The villa is listed at INR 2,100 per night on the booking page, for up to 4 adults and 1 child, and the rate includes GST. Each extra child (up to 4 children in all) is INR 400 per night. The final amount is confirmed by the host by email.

How do I book the villa?

Open the booking page, choose your check-in and check-out dates on the calendar, select the villa, enter your details and send the request. A booking is a request: your dates are held only after the host confirms by email. A one-page summary of your request can be saved as a PDF. Booking page: https://www.bwstays.com/bwstays-booking.html

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in from 2 PM, check-out by 11 AM.

How can I check availability?

The calendar on the booking page shows booked and closed dates (dates already requested may show as Requested), and you can choose dates from today up to one year ahead. A requested date is not confirmed yet. The host confirms every booking by email.

How many people can stay in the villa?

The villa takes up to 4 adults and up to 4 children (0 to 12 years); the booking page does not accept more. The nightly rate covers 4 adults and 1 child; each extra child is INR 400 per night (extra bed). The villa has 2 beds and 1 bathroom, a garden and private parking, and pets are allowed.

What amenities does the villa have?

EV charging, private parking, eco-friendly, premium utensils, a kitchen set, bonfire and music, television, online games, high-speed WiFi, security, pet friendly, bathroom amenities, air conditioning, a mountain view and a garden.

Is the villa suitable for a workation?

High-speed WiFi and air conditioning are listed among the villa amenities, so it suits remote work. The WiFi speed is not published, so ask the host if you need a specific bandwidth.

Where is the villa located?

In Kalpetta, Wayanad, Kerala (PIN 673121), close to the city centre. The Places to visit page lists attractions with their driving distance from Kalpetta (https://www.bwstays.com/all-locations-wayanad.html), and the Places to eat page lists restaurants and cafes (https://www.bwstays.com/all-fooding-wayanad.html).

Which places can I visit near the villa?

The site lists waterfalls, view points, lakes, temples, museums, plantations, wildlife sanctuaries and treks around Wayanad, each with a page showing timings, rating and distance from Kalpetta. Nearby EV charging stations and fuel stations are shown on each place page. Start at https://www.bwstays.com/all-locations-wayanad.html

Is there EV charging near the villa?

EV charging is listed among the villa amenities. Every place page also shows nearby public and private charging stations and fuel stations on a map; their positions are approximate.

Can I use the website without internet?

Yes, partly. The website can be installed on a phone (Add to Home Screen) and keeps the pages you have opened, the places, food, charging and fuel lists and the policies available offline. Live availability, sending a booking request, the chat assistant, the weather and the currency rates need an internet connection.

What is the concept behind Black and White Stays?

Colive, Cowork and Coplay: a villa in Kalpetta for staying with family or friends, working remotely, and exploring Wayanad.

Summarize BW Stays Wayanad in one sentence

Black and White Stays is a single villa in Kalpetta, Wayanad for staycations and workations, for up to 4 adults and 4 children, listed at INR 2,100 per night.
