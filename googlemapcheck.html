The Wayanad Glass bridge is spotted at a distance of 900m from Thollayiram Kandi and is approximately 13km from Meppadi.

The 900 Kandi Glass Bridge delivers a captivating experience, blending heart-pounding adventure with breathtaking views of nature. Suspended high above lush valleys and green landscapes, this glass bridge offers a one-of-a-kind journey that will leave you in awe.
